# Platform specs

| Platform | Aspect ratio | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 1:1 or 4:5 (16:9 OK) | 15–45 s | autoplays muted; B2B proof matters |
| X | 16:9 or 1:1 | 20–90 s | muted autoplay; hook in 2 s |
| Product Hunt | 16:9 | 45–60 s (≤90 s) | YouTube link; value prop in 5 s |
| Website hero | 16:9 loop | 20–60 s | muted autoplay; no CTA needed |
| Changelog / docs / in-app | 16:9 or 1:1 | 5–20 s | loop, small file (<3 MB GIF/MP4) |
| Reels / TikTok / Shorts | 9:16 1080×1920 | 6–30 s | safe zone below |
| YouTube | 16:9 | 60 s+ | VO versions do well |

**9:16 safe zone (union of Reels, TikTok and Shorts):** keep key text inside y ≈ 270–1250 px and ≥120 px from both sides. Use the bottom third for decoration only.

**Cutdowns:** master → 15 s 9:16 → 6 s bumper. Each is a separate composition, not a crop.
